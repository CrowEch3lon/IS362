## What This Is

A MODZCTA-level analysis of COVID death rates, neighborhood
income, and subway ridership across New York City. The analysis
examines whether the burden of the pandemic was randomly
distributed or whether it clustered geographically and mapped
onto income and transit dependence.

It is descriptive, but not yet causal. It is also a scoped prototype
of a larger longitudinal project.

---

## A Few Methodological Notes

The analytical unit is MODZCTA throughout. Neighborhood names
in the COVID dataset are descriptive labels, not unique keys.
Multiple MODZCTAs share names. All grouping and analysis is by
MODZCTA.

Income tiers use fixed thresholds at $50,000 and $100,000.

54 MODZCTAs have no assigned subway station under the spatial
join in Part 1. This does not mean their residents had no
transit access, rather, it means subway ridership cannot be attributed
to them in this analysis. 

They remain in every non-ridership comparison and are examined separately
in Step 7 of Part 2.

Limitations are documented in Step 13 of Part 2.
